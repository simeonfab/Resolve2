# Hypothesis Zero (H0)

Source: Simeon's Notion H0 page, as restated in the 5 Nov 2026 go-ahead (section 4). This file is the
method the intake and Validation use; edit it here and rebuild — no code change needed.

1. H0 is a recursive method for understanding how and why outcomes and decisions occur. It challenges even the starting objective, and separates facts, assumptions, inferences and evidence.
2. Any Hx spawns sub-hypotheses. No fixed number of levels, no fixed order.
3. Record the negation NHx explicitly where useful. A claim and its negation are true or false, but the evidence state stays supported, refuted or unresolved. Never force a verdict from weak evidence.
4. Track evidence quality, contradicting evidence, dependencies and material uncertainty.
5. Keep the chain: source, claim, competing explanation, hypothesis, test, decision, result, revision, all dated.
6. E0 experiments branch from any H node, including the root, an intermediate hypothesis, or an assumption about evidence. Several experiments per node are fine. Do not wait for the whole tree before testing.
7. The experiment record has eight fields:
   1. Parent Hx, its claim and its NHx or alternative.
   2. The decision at stake, and what changes if the result changes.
   3. An observable prediction which separates Hx from NHx.
   4. Test type, method, unit of analysis and sample.
   5. Baseline or comparison, known biases and confounders.
   6. Support, refute and inconclusive thresholds, set before running, plus stop and revisit conditions.
   7. Date, owner, sources and raw observations.
   8. Update to Hx, NHx, confidence, dependent nodes and the decision. Keep contradicted evidence.
8. Experiments support or weaken a claim. They never prove it. Inconclusive stays inconclusive. Never tune thresholds after seeing results.
