## Voice capture (E2): the approach I propose

**Local Whisper (whisper.cpp, "base.en" model), with Windows voice typing kept as the fallback.**
- Cost: free. No account and no per-use charge.
- Download: about 142 MB for the model, once, only after the person says yes in Settings. The app grows by about 2 MB.
- Privacy: audio never leaves the computer. It is recorded, turned into text on the machine, then thrown away.
- Speed: a few seconds of CPU for a short clip.
- Risk: building the C++ part with this PC's Rust toolchain is unverified until I try it.

The other options I checked on this PC:
- Windows voice typing (what is there now): free with no download, but audio goes to Microsoft and the app can't show a true recording state.
- Classic Windows speech (offline, installed): free with no download, but poor accuracy.
- The newer Windows speech service: it needs online speech turned on, so audio goes to Microsoft.
