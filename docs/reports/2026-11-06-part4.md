## What changed, in plain English

### Part 3 fixes (branch `intake/fixes`, merged)

1. **Home no longer overflows.** Every row wraps inside its card. At 1280 px and 1920 px nothing runs off the right edge or overlaps (`p3-home-1280.png`, `p3-home-1920.png`, `nav-1920-home.png`). The "What Resolve has heard" panel used to overflow at 1280 px. Its words now wrap under each label (`03-intake-questions.png`).
2. **A new user lands in the intake inside the main window.** A brand-new data folder opens straight on "What's your name?" in the main window, not a second window (`01-launch.png`). The steps to "Open my project" are `01` to `10` (list below). Test Intake still opens its own window with its own sandbox.
3. **The confirm screen has two actions only.** They are "This looks right, show me the proposal" and "Something's wrong, let me change it" (the second puts the cards into edit mode). The tick is gone. Editing the outcome counts as confirming it (`04-intake-confirm.png`).
4. **Confirm card text** starts with a capital letter and is written as "you", never "the user". It is never cut off mid-word, and the cards grow to fit (`04-intake-confirm.png`).
5. **The ending follows the original Resolve:**
   - Your next move, with why (`05-intake-proposal-0.png`).
   - Roadmap themes, one card at a time, with "Yes, add to my roadmap" or "Not quite" (`06-…-1.png`).
   - "Here's where to begin": the first week in order (`07-…-2.png`).
   - The whole picture, Now / Next / Later, then Adopt (`08-…-3.png`).
   - Building your project (`09-bridge.png`), then the reveal with "Open my project" (`10-reveal.png`).
   - "See and edit everything →" opens the full editor you had before.
   - A theme you leave off the roadmap is now kept on the project's cut list.
6. **Speed** (measured from a real run; table below):
   - The AI work now runs in the background while you read or type: sorting each answer, the understanding, and the proposal.
   - Every wait shows what is happening and a seconds counter.
   - No question waits more than 15 s. At 15 s Resolve shows its fixed next question instead.
   - With an Anthropic API key, the app uses the API rather than starting the Claude Code program on every call.
7. **Ollama:** qwen3 is the default and the recommended model. When llama3.2 is selected, a note says "Fine for trying the flow, weak for real projects".
8. **Workspace switcher:** the only entry for a new workspace is "+ Start something new with Resolve". It runs the intake full screen, and Adopt creates a new workspace with that project as its first project. "Start with Resolve" is gone from the sidebar. "New project" (Home, Projects, the project picker) still opens the intake.

### Part 4: navigation ported from Control Centre (branch `nav/control-centre-port`, merged)

The sidebar now follows Control Centre (`nav-1280-home.png`):

- **Top:** workspace switcher, Undo ↶ and Redo ↷, then Add task and Ask AI.
- **Workspace:** Home, Projects, Calendar.
- **Product management:** a project picker ("All projects" by default), then Overview, Roadmap, Tasks, Frameworks and Knowledge.
- **Collaboration:** AI Team and Handoffs.
- **Bottom:** Search, Settings, Help and the save status.
- Unfinished intakes stay listed under the groups while one exists.

What each part does:

- **Overview:** your project's "Do this next", progress, the ResolveOS actions, what matters now and the project context. "📄 Plan page" and "☰ Tasks board" moved here from the old cockpit bar (`nav-1280-overview_p.png`).
- **Roadmap:**
  - Now / Next / Later "by phase" (`nav-1280-roadmap_p.png`), or "by theme" with each theme's tasks.
  - Each theme shows its place in the sequence ("Step 3"), its basis and task progress.
  - From a theme's ⋯ menu you can set what it comes after (dependency), a validation gate (it waits until the chosen hypothesis is Supported) and its status. ← / → move it between phases.
  - Below the lanes: Milestones, Dependencies & blockers, and "Coming up" (dated tasks in the next 14 days; this is where Upcoming feeds the roadmap).
  - No dates are invented; order comes from sequence.
  - "All projects" shows every project's roadmap side by side (`nav-1920-roadmap.png`).
- **Tasks:** with a project picked, that project's list and board. With "All projects", the old Inbox (now "Captured"), Today, Upcoming and Filters & labels appear as filters at the top (`nav-1280-tasks.png`).
- **Frameworks:** a small registry with Hypothesis Zero first, and the H0 tree editor (`nav-1280-frameworks_p.png`). "All projects" shows every project's tree.
- **Knowledge** (`nav-1280-knowledge-0_p.png` to `nav-1280-knowledge-4_p.png`), in tabs:
  - Brief & documents: the project brief, then every page and favourite, with the new-page and template menu that used to be in the sidebar.
  - Research: pages tagged "research", plus open questions and untested assumptions.
  - Cut list: what you chose not to do yet, and why.
  - Evidence & Records: hypotheses, experiments, decisions, evidence and the project's history, with links kept.
  - Advanced: databases.
- **AI Team:** General assistant, Product Director, Validation Lead, Growth Lead and Tech Lead (`nav-1280-team_p.png`). Each reads the real project context and, on Claude or ChatGPT, can change the workspace through the same tools as Ask AI. You can undo every change.
- **Ask AI** is the General assistant, in the same engine and the same conversation as on the AI Team screen (`nav-1280-ai.png`). Its placeholder and suggestions change with the screen you're on (Ask Resolve).
- **Handoffs:** create a brief "for" someone with "what they should do". It is kept as a Handoff record you can copy, mark Sent or Done, and reopen (`nav-1280-handoffs_p.png`). Behind it is Resolve 2's existing brief generator. The always-up-to-date brief with "Save as a page" is still there.
- **Undo / Redo** (buttons, and Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y when you're not typing): undo the latest recorded change (delete, complete, adopt, AI change). Redo reverses an undo, and a redo can itself be undone. A new change clears Redo.

## Deviations and open points (please confirm)

1. **The proposal can still take longer than 15 s on Claude Code.** It takes about 60 s on the subscription CLI. It now runs in the background from the end of the questions, so by the time you've read the confirm cards it is usually done. If you click straight through, you'll see the progress card count past 15 s. To shorten it I'd need to either use a faster model for the plan half (quality trade-off) or use the Anthropic API. **Unverified:** the API path was not measured, because there is no API key on this machine.
2. **AI Team is ported without its role editor.** Control Centre's "Manage roles" (choose a different AI per role, add your own roles) was not ported; all five roles use the AI chosen in Settings. Say if you want it in this round.
3. **Ask Resolve wording.** Home, Tasks and Roadmap use the wording from briefs/14-N8-global-bar.md (Dashboard, Work, Roadmap). Overview, Frameworks, Knowledge, Handoffs and Calendar follow the same pattern, but their wording is mine. The brief asks for this to be flagged, not guessed, so please confirm or change it in `src/ui/nav.rs`. On the AI Team screen the panel shows no suggestions, only "Ask the General assistant (the team chat is on this page)", because that screen has its own chat.
4. **Research** means pages tagged "research" under the project brief, plus open questions and untested assumptions. There was no separate research store, and I didn't add one.
5. **Undo / Redo** covers the changes Resolve 2 already records: deletes, completes, Adopt and every AI change. Typing in a page or a field is not part of it (text boxes keep their own Ctrl+Z).
6. **Colours.** The old cockpit screens (Overview, H0, records) still contain a few hard-coded colours from before this round. They are listed in RECONCILIATION §2.6, and I added none. One styling note: `wrap_label`, `wrap_link` and the card layout in `widgets.rs` changed in Part 3 (named in that commit message).
7. **The "← / →" phase buttons** on roadmap cards are small text buttons, not drag and drop.

## Test steps for you

1. Pull, build (`cargo build`), and run `target\debug\resolve2.exe --selftest`. It should end "153 passed, 0 failed".
2. **First run:** run `target\debug\resolve2.exe --data-dir %TEMP%\r2-fresh`. You land on "What's your name?" in the main window. Go through to "Open my project".
3. **Ending:** on the themes card, press "Not quite", then "Leave it off the roadmap". After Adopt, check Knowledge → Cut list.
4. **Projects:** in the sidebar, pick your project in the picker. Click through Overview, Roadmap (try "By theme" and a card's ⋯ menu), Tasks, Frameworks, Knowledge (all five tabs), AI Team and Handoffs.
5. **All projects:** set the picker back to "All projects". Tasks shows Captured / Today / Upcoming / Filters & labels; Roadmap and Frameworks show every project.
6. **Undo / Redo:** complete a task, press ↶ (or Ctrl+Z), then ↷ (or Ctrl+Y).
7. **Ask AI:** open Ask AI on Home and then on Roadmap; the placeholder and suggestions change. Ask something, then open AI Team → General assistant: it's the same conversation.
8. **Workspace switcher:** open the menu. It shows only "+ Start something new with Resolve" for new workspaces.
9. **Ollama:** in Settings → AI, choose Ollama with llama3.2 and check the note.
