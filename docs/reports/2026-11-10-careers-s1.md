## 1. What I built

- **The mode field.** `WorkspaceData.mode` in `src/model.rs`: PM (default), Careers, YGO. A missing mode loads as PM, so every existing workspace opens as PM with no migration. A PM workspace never writes its mode, so its file is saved exactly as before. A sync merge keeps the local mode. An intake carries its own mode too (same rules), because it runs before its workspace exists.
- **Per-mode tables, picked by the open workspace's mode.** One function per table; no view is copied per mode. PM's entries are today's tables, untouched. Careers' entries come from the Careers brief and canvas. YGO is a slot that returns nothing.

| Table | Where | Picked by | Reads it |
|---|---|---|---|
| NAV (sidebar groups, items) and the Ask Resolve wording on each item | `ui/nav.rs` | `nav(mode)` | the sidebar, `ask_copy()` (Ask bar) |
| FRAMEWORKS | `ui/nav.rs` | `frameworks(mode)` | `frameworks.rs` (registry strip), `knowledge.rs` (all-projects view) |
| KNOWLEDGE_TABS | `ui/nav.rs` | `knowledge_tabs(mode)` | `kb.rs`, `knowledge.rs` |
| GUIDE | `ui/help.rs` | `guide(mode)` | the Help page, Copy the guide as text |
| TARGETS | `resolve/journey.rs` | `targets(mode)`, via the intake's own mode | next question (AI and fixed set), coverage, classification |
| RAIL_STEPS | `ui/dashboard.rs` (now one table: name and line) | `rail_steps(mode)` | the intake rail, Home's Continue setting up card. The rail's copy in `widgets.rs` (INTAKE_STEPS) was a second copy of the same steps; it's now this one table. |
| AI Team roles and their actions | `resolve/team.rs` | `team(mode)`, `default_role(mode)`, `specialist_in(mode, id)` | the AI Team page (both layouts), each role's instructions |

- **Careers entries:** sidebar with YOUR SEARCH (Pipeline, My CV, Stats, Tasks, Interview recorder, Frameworks, Knowledge base); the Ask wording for every Careers screen, word for word; Frameworks (Gates, CV templates, Cover letter, Interview prep, Offers and negotiation, Improvement playbook, Advice, each with who reads it); Knowledge tabs; 8 intake targets; the rail (Get started, Bring it in, Confirm understanding, Your kit); the AI Team (General assistant, Scout, Reviewer, Tailor, Coach, Negotiator, each with its action); the Careers guide's 12 sections.
- **No Careers screens.** Nothing in the app makes a Careers workspace yet. If one is opened (only a test can make one), Pipeline, My CV, Stats, Interview recorder, Frameworks and Knowledge base show one holding card, "<Screen> comes in a later step", built from the existing empty state. PM never reaches it.
