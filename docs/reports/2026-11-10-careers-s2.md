## 1. Check and report first

- **How Adopt works today.** `adopt_step` runs one step per bridge tick into the open workspace's data; `adopt_all` runs them all. For "Start something new", `adopt_begin` already creates a workspace (`reg.create`), moves the intake into it, switches to it and runs the bridge there. **Careers Adopt** uses that path and sets the new workspace's mode to Careers before saving (`adopt_begin_careers`). `journey::adopt_step` hands a Careers intake to `careers::adopt_step` (six steps, matching the six bridge lines), so the bridge, Undo and the reveal are shared. Inside a Careers workspace, + New project adopts into the same workspace as a second search.
- **TARGETS.** The Careers targets fit the table's shape (key, label, fixed question, what's heard). Only the fourth column changes meaning: Careers uses gate, preference or question, which the heard panel labels Hard gate, Preference and Question. No new fields.
- **Attach.** The app reads .md, .txt, .docx and .pdf with its own readers (no PDF or Word crate; .docx via the zip crate already there). **A real exported PDF doesn't read cleanly**: the brief's own PDF (made by Google Docs) came out as image and colour-profile bytes, not text (checked in a sandbox). A LinkedIn PDF export is likely to fail the same way: unverified, I have no LinkedIn export to test. Reading modern PDFs properly needs a PDF crate. **May I add one?** (for example `pdf-extract`).
- **Intake 12 copy.** Uses the PM screen's wording: "You've reached your AI limit for now." The canvas line is not used.

## 2. What I built

1. **02a**: a fourth path card, "I'm looking for a job", live, with a New tag. Choosing it runs the Careers intake; the top bar shows "Careers".
2. **02b**: "Tell Resolve what job you're looking for." The eight headings (Use a template), Try an example, Bring it in from another AI chat, and "Attach your CVs and LinkedIn" with the file chips.
3. **02c**: the careers pull-in prompt (the eight headings, first person, Not covered), Copy prompt, paste the answer back.
4. **03**: the Careers targets, cap 8, guided by gate coverage (a Careers prompt in place of H0). Targets the notes or files answer are skipped, and the screen says how many ("Question 3 of 8 · Skipped 2 …"). A broad answer gets one push, "That covers a lot. Which one first?", once per target. The heard panel groups Hard gates, Preferences and Questions.
5. **04 and 04b**: the five cards; the CV card has Strengths, Gaps, What to fix and **Rebuild my CV**, which opens the CV part of the full proposal and rebuilds the CV from the attached file (one call). **The rebuilt CV saves into My CV**: on Adopt it becomes the Master CV record. Edit makes every card editable with Save changes and Cancel.
6. **05**: "We're not going to guess." and the three-months question, when the target still covers many jobs.
7. **06**: the dark Do this first card with its why, and the Get your kit ready / Start applying / Prepare for interviews strip (the middle line carries the weekly target).
8. **07**: one gate at a time, each with its **Hard gate or Preference tag and the reason it matters**, the counter, Yes keep this gate, Not quite (edit, or leave it out), Make it a preference, and Your gates filling on the right.
9. **08**: the five first-week tasks with their days, as the brief lists them.
10. **09**: the search name, the six blocks, Do this first, "Adopt, start my job search →" and "Edit the full proposal →".
11. **The proposal editor**: the Careers left nav (Target, Gates, CV templates, Cover letter, Evidence bank, LinkedIn fixes, First week); templates with Default and Set as default and an editable headline; your CV.
12. **10 and 11**: the bridge's six lines, bound to the real Adopt steps; the reveal "Your job search is ready." with gates, templates and proof points, Do this first, and **"Open my job search →", which lands on Home**.
13. **Adopt** creates a new workspace in Careers mode (default "Job search", renamed "Job search 2" if taken) with the search, gates, CV templates, cover letter template, master CV, evidence bank, LinkedIn fixes, weekly target, five dated tasks, the transcript and the attached files.
14. **No AI**: the fixed Careers question set runs; the cards and the kit use only the person's own words (no invented gates, reasons, templates or proof points).
15. **Data**: new record kinds for gates, CV templates, the cover letter template, the master CV, proof points, LinkedIn fixes and the weekly target, kept out of PM's kind lists. The intake carries a `career` part (cards, kit, rebuilt CV). Both additive.
16. **The build check** now photographs the café sample's Home in a sandbox (your tweak from Step 1).

One model call = one job: next question, sorting an answer, the cards, the kit (two halves side by side) and the CV rebuild are separate calls. Tests use the labelled stand-in server.

**Where the build differs from the reference screens, and why**
- **The frame is the built Pack A frame** (dark rail with the steps' lines, dark top bar), as the brief says: "the Pack A frame unchanged … Only content changes." The canvas draws a lighter rail.
- **02a stays a list of four cards, not a 2 × 2 grid.** The welcome is shared with PM; a grid would redraw PM's screen. The PM cards keep their built wording.
- **Buttons keep the built styles** (the main action is dark on most steps, amber on 07 and 11, as in PM). The canvas uses amber throughout.
- **03's skipped line** reads "Skipped 2 your CVs and notes already answered": the canvas says "your CVs", but the skips also come from the 02b notes.
- **09's evidence count** is 4 in the sample (the canvas's 4 written-out proof points); the canvas says 24.
- **The editor has a Workspace name field** on Target (default "Job search"). The brief says the user names the workspace; no screen draws where.
- **07's reason** when a gate came from the no-AI path and has none: "You said this in the intake. Add why it matters in the full proposal." (my wording).
