### Known differences from Pack C

- **Numbers** come from the sample data, so they differ from the canvases in places (for example "20 records" where the canvas says 26, and 3 pages and files where it says 9).
- **Overview**: the empty state's two buttons are stacked, not side by side.
- **Roadmap**: + Add a theme opens a card above the lanes (the canvas doesn't draw it); theme cards have a ⋯ menu for phase, order, gate and status.
- **Frameworks**: the node editor has an "All fields…" link to every field of the record (the canvas shows only the main ones).
- **Proposal editor**: it starts with a Name and summary card the canvas doesn't have; the canvas joins hypotheses and experiments in one card, I show experiments in their own card under it, with their prediction and thresholds.
- **Knowledge base**: "Resolve searches everything here" where the canvas says "Resolve reads everything here". The AI is given search hits from the workspace, not every page, so "reads" would overstate it. Tell me if you want the canvas wording.
- **Page editor**: the old ◂ back and ⋯ bar is gone; favourite, change icon and Ask AI moved into the ⋯ menu. Sub-pages, backlinks and linked tasks stay under the text, which the canvas doesn't show.
- **Databases**: the old ◂ / ⋯ strip stays above the page (copy as CSV, save as template, delete); inside the table, the cells, column menus, Table/Board, Filter and Search are the earlier ones, so the table looks busier than the canvas (type icons in the column heads, row buttons).
- **AI Team**: the dates in Earlier come from the sample's real timestamps against the pinned clock, so they read "7 Nov" and "Today" rather than Tue and Mon.
- **Handoffs**: the brief's What already exists lists everything the project has on the request, so it is longer than the canvas's two sentences. The AI's rewrite is shorter; screenshots show the plain brief.
- **Everywhere**: the .98 and .97 press and lift scale effects are left for the polish pass, as you decided.

## 5. What I couldn't do, and why

- **The mic** on AI Team and Handoffs opens Windows voice typing (Win+H). A screenshot can't show speech being typed, so this is **unverified** until you try it.
- **A real AI writing a Proposed change line.** The card, Add it and Not now are self-tested with a written message; I didn't run a live model for it. Unverified.
- **The Proposed change card only appears for roles that can't change your workspace** (Ollama and OpenAI-compatible models). With Anthropic, OpenAI cloud or a CLI, roles still change things directly with Undo, as before Phase 4. Making every role propose instead would change how those work, so I left it for you to decide.
- **The CSV failed-row rule is mine**, as reported in the checks: more cells than columns, or an unreadable date or number. It's one function if you want another.
