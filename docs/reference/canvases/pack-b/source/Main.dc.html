Design Pack B · app shell and daily screens · desktop 1280 × 800

A dark rail, a light room, one bar to ask anything

The sidebar is the only dark surface in the app, the same nav-bg as the intake. It carries you from the intake's dark into the light work area. Every screen sits in the light area and shares one Ask Resolve bar.
