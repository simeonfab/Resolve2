Your first week

Here's where to begin.

Built straight from your roadmap. The first concrete moves, in the order they matter.

  1. 1
    Up next
    Reconstruct 12 months of hours per café
    For each of your three cafés, list the hours actually worked in each of the last 12 months. Use your timesheets, invoices or diary, not memory.
  2. 2
    Mark the busiest and quietest months
    For each café, find the highest-hour month and the lowest, and note what drove the peak (year-end, VAT quarter). This is the spike a flat fee must survive.
  3. 3
    Model the flat fee against real hours
    Set an hourly rate you'd accept, multiply by average monthly hours, and check what margin £150 leaves.
  4. 4
    Draft the fixed-fee offer for two clients
    A one-page offer for the two owners who said they'd prefer a fixed fee: monthly price, what's included, how year-end is handled.
  5. 5
    Define the monthly receipt handover
    What each café sends you and when, so records stay current and the year-end shoebox never forms.

Nothing here is final. You can change any of it once your project is open.
